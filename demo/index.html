VibeProtocol

VibeProtocol demo

An open protocol that lets any app be modded by its own users, safely. A user makes a wish, their AI writes a small sandboxed program called a vibe, and the app runs it inside a box it controls.

The tour

  1. In LeapTerm, pick the wish “Show disk usage for my servers, red above 80%”. Watch the scripted AI read what LeapTerm offers, write the vibe against sample data, and preview it.
  2. Check the generated permission label (Reads, Shows, Cannot) and press Allow and add.
  3. Disk Watch runs live in the panel. Open its ⋯ menu and press Inspect to see the opaque handles it received instead of server names, plus a ledger of what it read.
  4. Pick the second wish, “Make it red above 90% and show only the worst three”, to update Disk Watch to version 2 with no new permissions.
  5. In the agent chat, ask “Why is db-1 slow?”. The reply embeds a live CPU and memory view marked Temporary; press Pin to Vibe Space to keep it.
  6. Open the ⋯ menu on Disk Watch, press Share…, and choose Open as a teammate to see the label again and run it against the teammate’s servers.
  7. Switch to Moments: run “Who are my top fans?” (which ranks followers without identifying them), test a Disk Watch link to see it refused safely, and install Focus timer from Portable vibes in both apps.
  8. Manage, edit, and title your vibes: open a card’s ⋯ menu to Rename, Edit… (remix by wish or tweak code with a live sandbox test), restore earlier versions, or pause, reorder, filter, and undo deletes.
  9. Graceful when data sources go offline or are revoked: disconnect Telemetry feed or take db-1 offline in LeapTerm, or turn off Follower likes access in Moments — cards keep their last reading calmly, let you preview sample data, and recover automatically when the source returns.

What is real here

  • Real: the sandbox, the label, sealed values (plus capability checks, the read ledger, share links, and two hosts on one kit).
  • Scripted: the AI and the data (including the agent’s replies).
  • Stand-in: a browser sandbox instead of Wasm.
